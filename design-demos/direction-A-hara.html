About the test

About the test

The screen holds still, then changes colour. Click — or tap — the moment it does. Five rounds; your median time is the score. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for: the whole test runs in this browser tab, and the result stays on your device.

Reaction time depends on your equipment as much as on your nervous system. A mouse click is typically faster than a trackpad click, and both are faster than a touch tap. Treat the number as a reading, not a verdict.

A few questions
Is my score good? +

The published median for this kind of test is about 273 ms, measured across roughly 81 million samples. Faster than that, and you are quicker than most people. Much faster than that usually means you anticipated the change rather than reacted to it.

Is this an IQ test? +

No. It measures one narrow thing — how quickly you respond to a visual change. It is not a cognitive or intelligence score, and it says nothing about memory, attention or arithmetic.

Where do my results go? +

Nowhere. Results live in this browser’s local storage. There is no account, no upload, and no server.
